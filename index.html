About Sales by Kresser

Helping buyers purchase with confidence

When you purchase through Sales by Kresser, you are working directly with Joe Kresser, the founder and business owner.

After many years in business, Joe believes trust is earned through honesty, preparation and treating people with respect. He is well respected in both his church and community, and retirement has given him the opportunity to continue doing something he enjoys—helping others.

Whether presenting one of his own vehicles or helping someone else prepare an item for sale, the goal remains the same: provide detailed photographs, accurate descriptions, supporting documentation and straightforward communication without high-pressure sales tactics.

This website reflects that approach. Buyers can review the camper carefully and explore its condition and features before making the trip.

The Story

This 2006 Cadillac XLR-V has 78,850 miles and has been enjoyed and carefully cared for by its current owner for the past 13 years.

She is the second owner, purchasing the Cadillac in 2013 with 66,111 miles. Since then, she has driven it fewer than 13,000 miles, kept it garaged, maintained it, and—as she puts it—babied it.

The XLR-V combines Cadillac luxury with V8 performance and features an automatic transmission, power-retractable hardtop, leather seating, Bose sound system, and a wind deflector for top-down driving. The tires, brakes, and exhaust are in good condition.

The car rides and drives wonderfully and has been very dependable. As the owner has gotten older, she simply isn't using the Cadillac very much anymore, and she feels it is time for someone else to enjoy it.
